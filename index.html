Who is at Risk?

  • ✔️Everyone can get dengue regardless of age.
  • 👶Children and elders may face stronger symptoms.
  • ♻️People with previous dengue infections risk severe dengue.
  • 🧳Travelers to high-risk areas need extra protection.

Understanding Dengue

Dengue is a viral infection spread by Aedes aegypti mosquitoes. It causes high fevers, headaches, and severe body aches. In some cases it can become life-threatening, so stopping mosquitoes from breeding is essential.

The best news? Dengue is 100% preventable when we protect ourselves from mosquito bites.

🧭

How Does Dengue Spread?

  • 🌞Daytime biters: Aedes mosquitoes are most active early mornings and evenings.
  • 💧Stagnant water: They lay eggs in tiny pools like tires, pots, and gutters.
  • 🏠Indoor & outdoor: They thrive in homes, schools, and playgrounds.
  • ❌Not person-to-person: You cannot catch dengue just by being near someone sick.
🔄

Mosquito Life Cycle

Breaking the mosquito life cycle helps us stop dengue before it spreads. Watch every stage closely so we know where to act.

  1. Eggs: laid on water surfaces.
  2. Larvae: live in water for 7–10 days.
  3. Pupae: transform in just 2–3 days.
  4. Adults: emerge and can spread dengue.

Remove stagnant water weekly to break the cycle.

🌴

Dengue in Sri Lanka

Dengue cases rise during rainy seasons when warm, humid weather allows mosquitoes to multiply. Communities must keep their surroundings clean and clear of water to reduce the risk.

Working together, schools and families can keep outbreaks under control.

🩺

Dengue Symptoms

Learn to recognize the warning signs and know when to seek help. Spot the symptoms early to protect yourself and your loved ones.

🩹

Common Symptoms

Dengue symptoms usually begin 4–7 days after being bitten by an infected mosquito and can last up to a week.

🌡️

High Fever

Sudden high fever (40°C/104°F) that comes on quickly.

🤕

Severe Headache

Intense pain behind the eyes and across the forehead.

🦴

Joint & Muscle Pain

Severe pain in muscles, joints, and even bones.

👀

Pain Behind Eyes

Soreness when moving your eyes or looking around.

🤢

Nausea & Vomiting

Feeling sick, loss of appetite, or frequent vomiting.

🌺

Skin Rash

Small red spots appearing 2–5 days after the fever.

⚠️

Warning Signs – Seek Medical Help Immediately!

These symptoms can indicate severe dengue, which is a medical emergency.

  • 🩸
    Severe abdominal pain or persistent vomiting
  • 🩹
    Bleeding from nose, gums, or in vomit/stool
  • 😮‍💨
    Difficulty breathing or rapid breathing
  • 💧
    Fluid accumulation in chest or abdomen
  • 😴
    Lethargy, restlessness, or extreme fatigue
  • 🥶
    Cold or clammy skin or sudden dizziness
🏥 If you notice any of these warning signs, go to a hospital immediately!
💚

What Should You Do?

If you think you have dengue, follow these steps right away.

  1. Visit a doctor quickly for proper diagnosis and treatment.
  2. Rest and drink plenty of fluids (water, coconut water, ORS).
  3. Take paracetamol for fever (avoid aspirin or ibuprofen).
  4. Monitor symptoms closely and watch for warning signs.
  5. Prevent mosquito bites to stop spreading the virus.
  6. Follow your doctor's instructions carefully.
💡

There is no specific medicine for dengue, but with early detection and proper medical care most people recover fully within two weeks.

🌿

Recovery Tips

Take care of your body as it heals from dengue.

  • Get plenty of rest and sleep.
  • Drink lots of fluids to prevent dehydration.
  • Eat nutritious, easy-to-digest foods.
  • Use mosquito nets while resting.
  • Avoid strenuous activities until fully recovered.
  • Complete all prescribed medications.
  • Attend all follow-up appointments.

Prevention Tips

Simple steps to protect yourself and your community from dengue.

✨

Remember: Prevention is 100% Possible!

By following these simple steps, we can eliminate mosquito breeding sites and protect ourselves from bites.

💧

Remove Stagnant Water

Mosquitoes need water to breed. Remove all standing water sources.

Indoor Containers

  • Empty flower vases weekly
  • Clean water storage containers
  • Cover water tanks tightly
  • Check under sinks and bathrooms

Outdoor Areas

  • Empty old tires and bottles
  • Clean roof gutters regularly
  • Remove water from plant pots
  • Fill tree holes with sand

Garden Items

  • Turn buckets upside down
  • Store gardening tools properly
  • Change pet water bowls daily
  • Clean bird baths frequently

Waste Management

  • Dispose of trash properly
  • Cover garbage bins tightly
  • Don't let water collect in cans
  • Recycle or remove old items
🛡️

Protect Yourself from Bites

Stay safe by combining protective clothing, repellents, and home safety.

Wear Protective Clothing

  • Long-sleeved shirts and long pants
  • Light-colored clothing (mosquitoes prefer dark colors)
  • Covered shoes and socks
  • Dress carefully during peak biting hours (morning & evening)

Use Mosquito Repellents

  • Use mosquito coils or electric repellents indoors
  • Reapply repellent every few hours
  • Choose child-safe repellents for kids
  • Follow instructions on repellents carefully

Protect Your Home

  • Install window and door screens
  • Use mosquito nets while sleeping
  • Use air conditioning when possible
  • Keep windows and doors closed at dawn and dusk
🤝

Community Action

Dengue prevention works best when the whole community participates.

Neighborhood Clean-up

Organize community clean-up drives to remove breeding sites in common areas, parks, and public spaces.

School Awareness

Teach students about dengue prevention. Check school premises for mosquito breeding sites weekly.

Spread the Word

Share prevention tips with family, friends, and neighbors. Everyone's participation makes a difference!

Work Together

Coordinate with local health authorities for fogging and awareness campaigns in your area.

🗓️

Weekly Prevention Checklist

Keep dengue away with a quick weekly routine.

Do This Every Week:

  • Check all water containers and empty if necessary
  • Clean flower vases and change water
  • Inspect outdoor areas for stagnant water
  • Cover water storage tanks properly
  • Dispose of trash and recyclables
  • Check for and repair any leaking pipes
